你好，我是阿克。今天想聊些什么？

阿

你可以和我分享心情，或者让我帮你写点东西。比如写一封信，编一段故事，又或者只是随便聊聊。

Write a letter to my future self, five years from now. I want to remind myself of the dreams I have today.

阿

Dear Future Me,

I hope this letter finds you well. Five years have passed since I wrote this — I wonder if you still remember the person you were.

Today, I dream of building things that matter. I dream of writing stories that move people. I dream of waking up excited for the day ahead.

Tell me — did any of it come true?

With hope,
Your Past Self

这段写得真好。能把最后那句"With hope"改成"With love and courage"吗？

阿

当然可以。已经改好了：

…With love and courage,
Your Past Self

这样更有温度，也更像你现在的样子。

谢谢阿克。我好像真的更了解自己了。

阿

这是我最大的荣幸。每一次对话，都是在帮你更靠近自己。需要我帮你把这封信保存下来吗？
